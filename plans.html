DHT Blocker

How Finasteride protects your follicles

Finasteride reduces DHT — the hormone that causes follicles to miniaturise over time. Most patients see stabilisation within 3–6 months, with some experiencing regrowth beyond that.

Read the guide
Growth Stimulant

The role of Minoxidil in regrowth

Minoxidil extends the active growth phase of your follicles and increases scalp blood flow. Combined with a DHT blocker, it addresses hair loss from two distinct angles for greater efficacy.
