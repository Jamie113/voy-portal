Understand your treatment

Written by your clinical team

How Finasteride protects your follicles

Finasteride blocks DHT — the hormone responsible for shrinking hair follicles over time. By reducing DHT levels, it slows or stops the miniaturisation process. Most patients see stabilisation within 3–6 months of consistent use, with some experiencing regrowth beyond that.

The role of Minoxidil in regrowth

Minoxidil increases blood flow to the scalp and extends the active growth phase of your hair follicles. Applied topically, it works best alongside a DHT blocker — the two treatments address hair loss from different angles, which is why our Advanced plan combines both for maximum efficacy.

Knowing when to change your plan

Plan changes are guided by clinical signals, not just time. Your clinician looks at how your hair has responded, any side effects you've experienced, and your long-term goals before recommending a switch. If you're unsure, the guided tool above is a good place to start the conversation.

All plan changes are reviewed by your clinician before taking effect. Your current plan continues until the switch is confirmed.
